About Eco Glow Cleaning Solutions

Eco Glow Cleaning Solutions is an eco-friendly cleaning business that provides cleaning services for homes and small businesses.

The organisation focuses on providing safe, sustainable and effective cleaning solutions while considering the impact of cleaning activities on the environment.

Our History

Eco Glow Cleaning Solutions was founded in 2022 by Naledi Khumalo in Johannesburg.

The organisation started with three employees and has expanded its services across Gauteng.

Eco Glow was created to provide customers with cleaning services that are effective while supporting a more environmentally conscious approach to cleaning.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide safe, sustainable and effective cleaning services using environmentally friendly products.

We aim to help homes and small businesses maintain clean spaces while promoting environmentally responsible cleaning practices.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become South Africa's leading eco-friendly cleaning brand.

Eco Glow plans to grow its business through future product sales and franchising by 2030.

Who We Serve

Eco Glow Cleaning Solutions is designed to serve customers who are looking for effective and environmentally conscious cleaning services.

  • Homeowners between the ages of 25 and 50
  • Small businesses
  • Environmentally conscious individuals

Our Values

Our values guide the way Eco Glow approaches its cleaning services and its relationship with customers.

  • Sustainability: Supporting environmentally conscious cleaning practices.
  • Safety: Providing safe cleaning solutions for customers and their spaces.
  • Quality: Providing effective cleaning services for homes and small businesses.
  • Customer Service: Making it easy for customers to find information, enquire and book cleaning services.
