About Eco Glow Cleaning Solutions

Eco Glow Cleaning Solutions was founded in 2022 by Naledi Khumalo in Johannesburg.

The organisation started with three employees and focuses on providing safe, sustainable and effective cleaning services.

Eco Glow provides cleaning services for homes and small businesses. The organisation aims to use environmentally friendly cleaning products while helping customers maintain clean and comfortable spaces.

Eco Glow's mission is to provide safe, sustainable and effective cleaning services using environmentally friendly products.

The vision is to become South Africa's leading eco-friendly cleaning brand, with plans for product sales and franchising by 2030.
